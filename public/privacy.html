Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

CoachMohsen is a training app that padel coaches use to schedule sessions and share feedback with their players. This page explains what personal data the app keeps and why.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and, if you sign in with Google or Apple, the name and email that provider shares.
  • Training records your coach enters: sessions, attendance, skill ratings, comments and schedules.
  • Profile details your coach adds, such as name, phone number, level and playing side.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to let you sign in, show you your sessions and progress, and let your coach manage training. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Your coach and the app administrators. Players see only their own records. Coaches can keep private notes that players do not see.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with our hosting providers, Supabase (database and sign-in, located in the European Union) and Cloudflare (website delivery).

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your account and data at any time by contacting the app administrator. Deleted accounts can no longer sign in.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact your coach or the CoachMohsen administrator.

سياسة الخصوصية

كوتش محسن تطبيق يستخدمه مدربو البادل لجدولة الحصص ومشاركة التقييمات مع اللاعبين. نحفظ بريدك الإلكتروني وبيانات الدخول، وسجلات التدريب التي يدخلها المدرب، وبيانات ملفك مثل الاسم والجوال والمستوى. نستخدم هذه البيانات لتشغيل التطبيق فقط، ولا نبيعها ولا نستخدمها للإعلانات. يرى بياناتك مدربك ومديرو التطبيق، ويرى كل لاعب سجلاته فقط. تُحفظ البيانات لدى مزودي الاستضافة Supabase (في الاتحاد الأوروبي) وCloudflare. يمكنك طلب تصحيح بياناتك أو حذف حسابك في أي وقت عبر مدير التطبيق.
